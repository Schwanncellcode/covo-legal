Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 8, 2026

Covo ("we", "our", "us") operates the Covo mobile application. This policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your information.

Information We Collect

When you create an account, we collect your name, email address, date of birth, and profile information you choose to provide (bio, interests, profile photo, username).

When you use the app, we collect information about events you create or join, messages you send, communities you participate in, and friend connections you make.

We may collect device information including push notification tokens to send you alerts about events, messages, and friend requests.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to provide and improve the Covo app, including: creating and managing your account, displaying your profile to other users, facilitating event discovery and attendance, enabling messaging between users, sending push notifications, and processing payments for event entry fees.

Payment Processing

Payments are processed securely through Stripe. We do not store your credit card details. Stripe's privacy policy applies to payment transactions.

Information Sharing

Your profile information (name, username, bio, interests, profile photo) is visible to other Covo users. Your email address is never shared with other users. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

Data Security

We implement security measures including encrypted passwords, secure authentication tokens, rate limiting, and input sanitization to protect your data.

Blocking and Reporting

You can block other users to prevent them from viewing your profile or contacting you. You can report users for inappropriate behavior, and our team will review reports.

Account Deletion

You can delete your account at any time from the app settings. This will permanently remove your profile, messages, event history, and all associated data.

Children's Privacy

Covo is not intended for users under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 18.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes through the app.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at support@covo.social.
